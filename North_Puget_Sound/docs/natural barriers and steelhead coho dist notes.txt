notes on accessing statewide steelhead and coho predictive distribution layers and natural barriers datasets:

Predictive distribution service supplied for the North Puget Sound update:
https://services.arcgis.com/rcya3vExsaVBGUDp/arcgis/rest/services/Statewide_Dist_Models_1b66a3ddb471/FeatureServer
Steelhead Modeled Statewide: layer 0
Coho Modeled Statewide TW: layer 1
The steelhead layer publishes geometry, OBJECTID, MAX_UniqueCHID, and Shape__Length,
but no downstream routing or run-specific prediction fields. See README.md for
the reproducible analysis and unresolved-connectivity interpretation.

SWIFD-supported gap routing update:
https://geodataservices.wdfw.wa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/MapServices/SWIFD/MapServer/0
Winter and summer steelhead Presence/Spawning/Rearing are dissolved as routing
support. Artificial, Transported, and Presumed distribution are included;
Gradient Accessible categories are excluded. Both gap attachment locations must
be within 100 meters of eligible SWIFD and have a continuous SWIFD route.
Endpoint alignment links are topology only and excluded from habitat mileage.

Verified:
https://geodataservices.wdfw.wa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/ApplicationServices/FP_Sites/MapServer/5
 
Not Field Verified:
https://geodataservices.wdfw.wa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/ApplicationServices/FP_Sites/MapServer/6
 
FPDSI:
https://geodataservices.wdfw.wa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/ApplicationServices/FP_Sites/MapServer
Layer: Natural Barrier - Verified (ID: 5)
 
Here's how R access the verified:
 
library(sf)
 
layer <- "https://geodataservices.wdfw.wa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/ApplicationServices/FP_Sites/MapServer/5"

url <- paste0(

  layer, "/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=*&outSR=4326&f=geojson"

)
 
barriers <- st_read(url, quiet = TRUE)

head(st_drop_geometry(barriers))
Layer: Natural Barrier - Verified (ID: 5)
 
Heres how Python does:
 
import geopandas as gpd
 
layer = "https://geodataservices.wdfw.wa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/ApplicationServices/FP_Sites/MapServer/5"

url = (

    f"{layer}/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=*&outSR=4326&f=geojson"

)
 
barriers = gpd.read_file(url)

print(barriers. Head())
